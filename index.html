SRS SECTION 9.5 & 16 SPECIFICATION

Grievance Lifecycle Workflow

The system strictly enforces a sequential 5-stage lifecycle to eliminate overlooked complaints and ensure student visibility:

1. Submitted Actor: Student

Student lodges complaint with category, department, description, and proof. Unique ID generated.

2. Assigned Actor: Administrator

Admin delegates the grievance to the designated departmental faculty in-charge.

3. In Progress Actor: Faculty / Staff

Faculty investigates on site, enters official remarks, and requisitions repairs.

4. Resolved Actor: Faculty / Staff

Corrective action completed; faculty records official resolution statement and timestamp.

5. Closed Actor: Student / Admin

Student confirms satisfactory resolution and the record is officially archived.
